**unCommander** (Beta)

A two-pane, Midnight Commander-style file manager for unRAID. Browse two folders side by side and copy, move, rename, delete, compress, extract and compare files with the mouse, F-keys or taps on an iPad - with background jobs, Undo for every change, a restorable trash, batch rename, a viewer and editor, a picture editor, video and music players, playlists, a built-in terminal, and tools for duplicates, disk usage, empty folders, movie folders that lost their video (Radarr), TV shows with missing episodes (Sonarr) and music checks (Lidarr).
